## Final human gate

1. Sign in/register on the official CLOCK IN portal.
2. Start a Ground Relay project submission.
3. Use `docs/submission-copy.md` for project text.
4. Attach the physically verified APK.
5. Supply the public demo-video URL.
6. Attach the pitch deck requested by the form.
7. Confirm all previewed fields and links.
8. Do not press the final submit button until the complete form has been reviewed once.

No mainnet deployment is required or authorized by this checklist. The submitted proof is the verified devnet build and physical Android demonstration.
